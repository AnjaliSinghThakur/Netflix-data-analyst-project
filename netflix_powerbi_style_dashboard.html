Executive Summary

  • 7,770 titles across 81 producing countries analyzed (cleaned from 7,787).
  • Movies 69% / TV Shows 31%. TV Show additions grew from 184 (2016) to 697 (2020); share dipped to 25.5% in 2018, then recovered to 34.7% in 2020.
  • US is #1 producer (2,874); India #2 (956).
  • Top genre: Dramas. Top rating: TV-MA.
  • Peak year 2019; peak month December.
  • Recommendation: invest in international TV and family content to diversify beyond mature US catalog.
